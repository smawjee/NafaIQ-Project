Trust through clarity

Know what the product does—and what it does not claim.

NafaIQ is built as financial education and decision support. Important assumptions stay visible.

01

Explainable views

Illustrative comparisons are labeled, inputs remain visible, and nominal and adjusted outcomes are shown side by side.

02

Privacy-minded

Your portfolio is presented as private account data. NafaIQ’s interface does not use it as public social proof.

03

Decision support

AI context and screening tools help you investigate choices; they are not a promise of returns or personalized financial advice.

Market prices, rates, and adjusted-return examples may be delayed or illustrative in this prototype.

FAQ

Questions, answered clearly.

Still deciding? Explore the product without creating an account, or email the NafaIQ team.

What can I explore without an account?

You can explore market views, product previews, and the Haqeeqi Daulat experience before creating an account. Sign in when you want to save personal data.

How does NafaIQ handle financial data?

NafaIQ presents portfolio information as private account data. Review the current privacy terms before connecting or entering sensitive information.

Is NafaIQ Shariah compliant?

NafaIQ includes halal stock screening, a Zakat calculator, and Islamic savings goals to support values-aligned research. Screening results remain decision-support information.

How much does it cost?

The core terminal is free to explore with no credit card required. Visit Plans for the current premium feature breakdown.

Can I install it like an app?

Yes. NafaIQ is a progressive web app on supported browsers. When installation is available, this page will show an install action; iPhone and iPad users receive platform-specific steps.

Is NafaIQ financial advice?

No. NafaIQ provides financial education, analytics, screening, and decision-support tools. You remain responsible for investment and financial decisions.
