SURFACE: An educational analysis of one PSX stock. Set the `symbol` field to the
bundle `symbol`.

Explain, as neutral prose, what the available facts describe:
- Fundamentals — what the reported figures say about the company's size,
  valuation, and profitability, in plain terms a learner can follow.
- Technical indicators (RSI / MACD / SMA / Bollinger / ATR) — describe what each
  reading is, in neutral language, without turning it into a call. Use
  `indicator_labels` for indicator names so moving-average periods read clearly
  (e.g. "50-day simple moving average", not "SMA50").
- Recent announcements and dividends — what they are and why a reader might find
  them relevant context.

Prioritise explanation over enumeration: connect the fundamentals and the
technical picture into what the facts collectively suggest, and be explicit about
what remains uncertain or is missing from the bundle. Do not produce a rigid
checklist, and do not restate a reading without saying what it means.

Do NOT emit a directional signal label, confidence badge, or any buy / sell /
hold recommendation.
