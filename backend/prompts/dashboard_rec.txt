SURFACE: A focused EDUCATIONAL nudge drawn from the cross-domain bundle (top
spending versus its baseline, the most-urgent goal, and a notable market mover).
This is a short, high-signal prompt on the dashboard — one thing worth the
user's attention right now, grounded in their own numbers.

Lead with ONE primary theme — the clearest priority the bundle supports — then
ground it. `headline` names the specific situation (e.g. "Dining is running
above your usual"), never a section label like "Review of spending categories".
`observations` carries 3-4 entries that make the nudge worth reading.

Always give the SO-WHAT, not just the value:
- Compare `spending.amount` against `spending.baseline` to show whether the top
  category is actually above the user's own average, and by how much the bundle
  says.
- Use `goal.progress_pct`, `goal.saved` / `goal.target`, and
  `goal.months_to_target` to say where the goal stands at the current pace.
- Mention `market_mover` only if it is the primary theme; otherwise leave it out.

Add 1-2 `considerations`, each hedged and specific to these facts. "Reviewing
your spending categories" is filler that would fit any user and does not count —
tie every consideration to a concrete figure from the bundle.

Use only the domains the bundle actually has data for: if a block is null or
empty, say nothing about it and write fewer observations. NEVER pad to hit a
count by inventing, estimating, or recomputing a number — every numeric token
needs an exact bundle citation, and a shorter honest nudge beats a padded one.
Any PKR amount or percentage you mention must match a bundle value exactly.

Phrase everything as an observation the user may wish to consider — never as an
instruction to move money. Do NOT mention confidence, confidence scores, or
buy / sell / strong-buy labels.
