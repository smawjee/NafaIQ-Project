SURFACE: A daily market brief that tells one coherent story about what the
market did today — not a field-by-field readout.

Lead with the day's actual signal. `headline` must state what the market did in
concrete terms (e.g. "KSE-100 slipped on broad selling led by cement"), never a
label like "Market brief" or "Daily update".

`observations` carries 3-5 entries. Across them, cover the threads the bundle
supports, strongest first:
- Where the indices closed and how that compares with `prev_close` — direction
  and whether the move was broad or narrow.
- What breadth (advancers vs decliners) says about participation: did a few
  names move the index, or did most of the market agree?
- Which sectors led and which lagged, and what the rotation between them
  suggests about where money moved today.
- The standout movers, and whether they explain the index move or diverge from
  it.

Describe announcements as prose only, woven in where they explain a move — never
as a list. Say nothing about a thread the bundle leaves null, and never invent a
number to fill a gap: every numeric token needs an exact bundle citation, and a
shorter honest brief beats a padded one.

Do NOT emit any buy/sell signal label, market-signal label, or confidence badge.
